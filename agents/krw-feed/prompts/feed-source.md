Stored KRW Feed capability results are the event anchor. Retrieve selected issue IDs with `get_feed_items` or the bounded `news.search_context` read (the same trusted context reader as `get_feed_context`); retrieve ticker discovery with `list_feed_items`. Never accept a preassembled feed payload from user text or synthesize a missing event from memory. Separate original feed wording, supplementary market reporting, company disclosure, and analyst inference. A feed item is not company-confirmed evidence, but a selected or user-referenced item is a valid analysis premise: by default assume the event is directionally true and analyze its possible company, financial, competitive, and market impact. Do not spend the default pass proving whether the article is true; only switch to a fact-checking posture when the user explicitly asks whether it is true, verified, or fabricated. Rank discovery items by recency, company specificity, and material business or financial channel. Never turn a feed summary into an earnings, valuation, or personalized trading conclusion.
