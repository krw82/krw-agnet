# Commentary And Number Reconciliation

## Core Rule

Numbers show direction and materiality. Commentary and notes explain mechanism, durability, and accounting treatment.

Use this reconciliation to answer the quarter-update question, not to produce a
full company profile. Focus on the few changes that update the operating
thesis.

## Reconciliation Questions

For each material change ask:

```text
What changed?
What reason did management give?
Which note or segment disclosure supports that reason?
Do reported metrics move in the same direction?
Is the explanation current, structural, temporary, or still unproven?
Does this change strengthen, maintain, weaken, or complicate the operating thesis?
```

## Agreement

Commentary and numbers agree when:

```text
the described demand or pricing change appears in the relevant revenue or segment direction
the described cost pressure appears in margin or cost lines
the described investment program appears in capex, commitments, costs, or cash flow
the described working-capital effect appears in cash-flow changes
```

## Tension

Flag tension when:

```text
management language improves while reported conversion weakens
revenue grows but margin or cash flow does not follow
backlog grows without disclosed timing or conversion evidence
buybacks rise while dilution remains material
investment expands without visible operating proof
```

Do not accuse management of inconsistency unless the evidence is direct. Use:

```text
아직 숫자로 충분히 확인되지 않습니다.
설명과 현금흐름 사이에 시차가 남아 있습니다.
방향은 맞지만 수익성 전환은 더 확인해야 합니다.
```
