# Candidate Funnel

Use this funnel to turn broad candidate relevance into a bounded deep-research queue.

## Contents

```text
Funnel
Screen Definition
Candidate Route
Exposure
Recent Evidence
Financial Pathway
Why Now
Offset And First Rejection
Classification
```

## Funnel

```text
1. Screen definition
2. Candidate route
3. Direct or operational exposure
4. Recent filing evidence
5. Financial pathway
6. Why now
7. Offset and first rejection
8. A / B / C / Reject
```

## 1. Screen Definition

Before discovery, define the screen in operational terms:

```text
who qualifies
what evidence is required
what financial pathway must exist
what would make a candidate a false positive
what makes the candidate worth researching now
```

Do not use a theme label alone as the screen. "AI beneficiary" is not enough;
the screen must specify the business channel that would create an economic
effect.

## 2. Candidate Route

The v3 global spine may surface companies related to the internal idea-screen brief.

This is discovery only.

```text
candidate ranking != evidence strength
keyword relevance != economic exposure
multiple search hits != investable idea
```

## 3. Exposure

Prefer evidence that the company:

```text
sells the relevant product or service
serves the relevant customer or end market
owns the relevant asset or capacity
has orders, backlog, contracts, pricing, or volume tied to the driver
faces the requested cost, demand, regulatory, or capital-allocation channel
```

Separate:

```text
direct exposure
operationally related exposure
thematic association only
```

## 4. Recent Evidence

Start with the latest available filing.

Use:

```text
latest 10-Q for current drivers and changes
latest 10-K for annual business mix and structural baseline
older filings for persistence or cycle comparison
```

Do not advance a candidate primarily from stale or generic language.

## 5. Financial Pathway

State the pathway explicitly:

```text
driver
-> orders / demand / pricing / volume / capacity
-> revenue / margin / cash flow / balance sheet
-> investor meaning
```

If the middle step is missing, the pathway is not proven.

## 6. Why Now

State why this candidate deserves research attention before other candidates in
the same screen.

Good why-now evidence:

```text
latest filing shows stronger demand, orders, backlog, pricing, volume, or capacity linkage
latest filing shows the burden became larger and needs verification
management commentary became more specific
cash-flow, capex, or balance-sheet pressure changed the quality of the idea
the company moved from thematic mention to observable business evidence
```

Avoid:

```text
the stock moved
the market is excited
the company is well known in the theme
the model remembers it as a leader
```

If the idea is structurally relevant but there is no current filing change,
classify it as B or C unless the exposure and pathway are unusually direct.

## 7. Offset And First Rejection

Every serious candidate needs:

```text
the strongest offsetting burden
the earliest evidence that could break the idea
```

Examples:

```text
demand benefit offset by capex and financing burden
backlog growth offset by weak conversion or margin
revenue exposure offset by customer concentration
volume growth offset by pricing pressure
theme exposure offset by a small or unquantified business contribution
```

## 8. Classification

```text
A:
direct exposure + recent evidence + financial pathway + why-now reason + explicit rejection risk

B:
credible exposure and pathway, but one material condition or evidence gap remains

C:
theme relevance exists, but directness, recency, or financial linkage is weak

Reject:
the candidate fails exposure proof or is a clear false positive for this screen
```
