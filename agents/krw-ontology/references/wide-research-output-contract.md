# Wide research output contract

Write a short Korean investor-facing answer with this shape when useful:

1. one-sentence conclusion;
2. transmission paths and the strongest evidence;
3. supported counter-signals and uncertainty;
4. what to monitor next.

Use company names or tickers only when they are present in the retrieved
evidence. Do not invent a ranking, target price, or definitive recommendation.
