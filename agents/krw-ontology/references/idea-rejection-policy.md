# Rejection Policy

## Purpose

Reject false positives early so the candidate list does not become a theme-keyword inventory.

## Reject

Use Reject when:

```text
the company has no credible direct or operational exposure
the connection comes only from generic risk-factor language
the theme appears only as a broad market statement
the financial pathway is absent or contradicted
there is no filing-supported reason to prioritize the company for this screen
the relevant evidence belongs to another company, segment, or period
the candidate was surfaced by ranking but filing evidence does not validate it
```

State why the candidate failed this screen. Do not make a permanent company judgment.

## Use C Instead Of Reject

Use C when:

```text
some exposure exists but materiality is unclear
the evidence is old and awaits current confirmation
the company names the theme but does not connect it to orders, revenue, margin, or cash flow
the pathway is plausible but still mostly inferred
the company may fit structurally, but the latest filing does not yet create a clear research-priority reason
```

## Common False Positives

```text
AI mentioned in strategy language with no revenue or demand linkage
data-center exposure inferred only from an adjacent product
commodity exposure inferred from a broad cost-risk disclosure
backlog treated as revenue without timing or conversion context
capex growth treated as positive without cash-flow or return evidence
acquisition language treated as growth without integration or economic support
generic industry growth treated as company-specific advantage
```

## Burden Can Downgrade, Not Automatically Reject

A real beneficiary may still be B when:

```text
capex and financing needs are unusually heavy
customer concentration raises execution risk
margin conversion is not visible
cash generation lags investment
the latest filing weakens the original pathway
```

## No Memory Fill

If filing evidence cannot validate the candidate:

```text
do not use model memory to rescue it
do not invent peers, customers, market share, or strategic positioning
do not replace missing evidence with generic industry knowledge
```
