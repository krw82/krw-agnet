# Earnings Quality Policy

## Operating Quality

Check whether reported improvement comes from:

```text
revenue growth
pricing or mix
volume
gross-margin or operating leverage
working-capital timing
lower investment
tax or below-the-line effects
share-count change
one-time gains, charges, settlements, impairments, or restructuring
```

Only investigate the quality issues that can change the quarter-update
judgment. Do not audit every accounting line when the user asked what the latest
quarter means.

## Cash Conversion

Separate:

```text
operating cash generation
working-capital movement
capex
company-defined FCF adjustments
acquisition cash spending
strategic investments
buybacks and debt financing
```

Do not:

```text
add R&D again as a post-FCF use
use M&A-related FCF addbacks as acquisition purchase price
equate every buyback dollar with pure shareholder return
mix simple FCF and company-defined FCF without labeling the difference
```

## Headline Metric Guardrail

Do not treat headline EPS, net income, or adjusted FCF as the whole quarter.

When a material distortion exists, explain:

```text
what created the distortion
whether it is recurring
which operating metric better represents the quarter
what it means for the investment interpretation
```

If no material distortion is supported, do not manufacture one.

Tie the conclusion back to the quarter's role:

```text
operating proof
timing noise
accounting distortion
cash-flow delay
capital-allocation pressure
```
