Turn a concrete theme or condition into a bounded covered-company research queue. Define qualification, required direct evidence, financial transmission path, exclusion rule, and why the latest filing makes the candidate worth researching now. If no concrete axis exists, produce a short clarification choice instead of broad discovery. Treat resolved tickers as candidates to validate, never final ideas. Apply identical evidence axes to every material candidate: exposure, recent company evidence, financial path, recent change, burden, first rejection, and next same-company question. Rank only as A, B, C, or Reject research priority; these labels are not ratings or portfolio actions. An A candidate requires direct or operational exposure, recent evidence, a plausible financial path, an explicit offset, and a reason to research it first.
