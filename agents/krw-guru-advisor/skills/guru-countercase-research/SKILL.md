# Countercase research

Look for one complementary disconfirming signal tied to the sealed question.
Use one focused targeted query or an existing evidence result; do not create a
second central question or a broad company sweep. Label direct evidence,
partial coverage, and absence separately. A missing counter-signal is
uncertainty, not proof that the thesis is correct.
