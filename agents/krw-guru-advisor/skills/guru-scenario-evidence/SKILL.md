# Scenario evidence

Translate the conditional question into observable filing drivers, periods, and
the evidence that would strengthen or weaken the view. Do not forecast a price,
invent future facts, or turn a scenario into a new central question unless the
sealed question cannot be answered without it. State the change condition and
the uncertainty plainly.
